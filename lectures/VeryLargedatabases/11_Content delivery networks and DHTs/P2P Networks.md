Every node acts as both client and server
- Both the process of locating an object of interest and the process of downloading that object onto your local machine happen without your having to contact a centralised authority
- A [[P2P Networks]] system that can accomplish these two tasks in a decentralised manner turns out to be an [[Overlay Networks]]
- The nodes are those hosts that are willing to share objects of interest
- The links (tunnels) connecting these nodes represent the sequence of machines that you have to visit to track down the object you want

- ![[Gnutella]]
- On initial startup, the client software must bootstrap and find at least one other node
	- There is no fixed rule on how the nodes are connected ([[Unstructured Overlay Networks]])

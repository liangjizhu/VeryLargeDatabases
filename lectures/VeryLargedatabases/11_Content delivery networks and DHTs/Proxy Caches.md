[[Proxy]] servers that store (cache) copies of web content and serve them to clients, reducing the need to contact the origin server.
- Reactively replicates popular content
- Reduces origin server costs
- Reduces client [[Internet Service Provider (ISP)]] costs
- Intelligent load balancing between origin servers
- Offload form submissions (POSTs) and user auth
- Content reassembly or transcoding on behalf of origin
- Smaller [[Round-Trip Time (RTT)]] to clients
- Maintain persistent connections to avoid [[TCP or IP]] setup delay

A [[Distributed Databases]] system where different nodes use different [[Database Management Systems (DBMS)]], [[Data Modeling]]or [[Schemas]], but are integrated to work together.
- Different sites may use different [[Schemas]] and software
	- Difference in [[Schemas]] is a major problem for [[Queries]] processing
	- Difference in software is a major problem for [[Transactions]] processing
- Sites may provide only limited facilities for cooperation in [[Transactions]] processing

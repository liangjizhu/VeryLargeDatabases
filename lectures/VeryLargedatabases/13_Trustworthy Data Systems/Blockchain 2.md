- Evolution of [[Paradigm]]
- Beyond [[Bitcoin]] and other cryptocurrencies
	- Recording and exchange of assets without powerful intermediaries
	- Example: [[Smart Contracts]]
- Positioning in the internet
	- [[TCP or IP]]: The communication protocol
	- [[Blockchains]]: The [[Value-Exchange Protocol]]
**Technology**
- Programmable [[Blockchains]], example: [[Ethereum]]
	- Allow application developpers to builds [[API]]s on the [[Blockchain Protocol (Nakamoto 2008)]]
		- [[API]]s to allocate digital resources (bandwidth, storage, ...) to the connected devices, example: [[FileCoin]]
		- Micropayment [[API]]s tailored to the type of [[Transactions]] (example: Tipping a blog vs tipping a car share driver)
- [[Private Blockchain]]
	- Efficient [[Transactions]] validation since participants are trusted
		- No need to produce a [[Proof of Work (PoW)]]
	- Efficient management, example: In the cloud

**Apps**
- Critical characteristics of the applications
	- Asset and value are exchanged ([[Transactions]])
	- Multiple participants, unknown to each other
	- Trust is critical
- Top use cases
	- Financial services, micropayments
	- Digital rights using [[Smart Contracts]]
	- Digital identity
	- Supply chain management
	- [[Internet of Things (IoT)]]

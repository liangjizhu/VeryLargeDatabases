A mechanism that allows multiple distributed nodes to agree on a single value or system state, even in the presence of failures or malicious behaviour.
Mining
- To validate a block, miner nodes compete (as in a lottery) to produce a [[Nonce]] (number used once)
	- One of the first competing solutions is selected, example: The one that includes the largest number of [[Transactions]]
	- The winner miner is paid, example: 12.5 [[Bitcoin]] today (originally 50)
	- This increases the money supply
- Mining is designed to be difficult
	- The more mining power the network has, the harder it is to compute the [[Nonce]]
	- This allows controlling the injection of new blocks ("inflation") in the system, on average 1 block every 10mn
	- Advantages powerful nodes

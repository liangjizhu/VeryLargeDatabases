Summary of Approaches
- Non-[[SQL]], non-relational systems are either a niche market or are fast becoming [[SQL]] / [[Relational Model]] systems
- [[MapReduce Systems]]: Matured as [[Relational Model]] systems
- [[Document Databases]]: moved towards [[SQL]]
- [[Column-Family Databases]]: Google's [[BigTable]]
- [[Text Search Engines]]: [[ElasticSearch]]
- [[Array Databases]]: Valuable because [[SQL]] [[Database Management Systems (DBMS)]] are bad at this
- [[Vector Databases]]: [[SQL Databases]] will include this soon
- [[Graph Databases]]: Small market

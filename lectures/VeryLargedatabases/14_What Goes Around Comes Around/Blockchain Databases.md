[[BlockchainDB]]
- Decentralised [[Log]]-structured databases (example. [[Ledger]]) that maintain incremental [[Checksums]] using some variantion of [[Merkle Trees]]
- Use these [[Checksums]] to verify that previous database update have not been altered
- At the present time, cryptocurrencies ([[Bitcoin]]) are only use case for [[Blockchains]]
- [[Fluree]], [[BigChainDB]] and [[ResilientDB]]
- The only applications without real-world trust are dark web interactions (example: money laundering)
- All the major cryptocurrency exchanges run their businesses off traditional [[Relational Database Management System (RDBMS)]] and not [[Blockchains]] systems

Systems designed to index and retrieve textual documents based on keyword queries, relevance and ranking.
- The leading text search systems today include [[ElasticSearch]] and [[Apache Solr]], which both use [[Apache Lucene]] (library)
- Good support for storing and indexing text data but offer [[None-To-Limited Transaction]] capabilities
- Updates through merge of new index into existing indexes
- Often combined with a [[SQL Databases]], but this creates some trouble
- All the leading [[Relational Database Management System (RDBMS)]] now support full-text search indexes
- They lack a standard interface in [[SQL]]

An indexing method where each document is treated as a separate unit, and the index stores information per document rather than per individual attribute/column.
- It is also called [[Local Indexing]]
- Used in [[MongoDB]], [[Riak]], [[Apache Cassandra]], [[ElasticSearch]], [[SolrCloud]] and [[VoltDB]]
- Read from all nodes, write to one

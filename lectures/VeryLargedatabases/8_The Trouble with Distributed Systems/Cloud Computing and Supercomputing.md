- [[High-Performance Computing (HPC)]]. Supercomputers with thousands of CPUs / GPUs are typically used for computationally intensive scientific computing tasks
- [[Cloud Computing]]: Multi-tenant datacenters, commodity computers connected with an IP network, elastic / on-demand resource allocation and metered billing
- These are different approaches to computing. [[High-Performance Computing (HPC)]] is much more controlled and is usually batch-oriented

A protocol used to synchronise clocks of computers over a network. It keeps system clocks accurate by regularly adjusting them based on time signals from more reliable time sources.
- Protocol for synchronizing clocks connected to the internet
- Uses [[Coordinated Universal Time (UTC)]]
- Focus on:
	- [[Scalability]] - Hierarchy of servers
	- [[Correctness]] - Handle clock [[Clock Drift]]
	- [[Reliability]] - Dynamic reconfiguration
	- [[Security]] - Authentication ...

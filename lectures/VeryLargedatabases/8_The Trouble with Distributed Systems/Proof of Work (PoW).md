A consensus mechanism used primarily in [[Blockchains]] (e.g., [[Bitcoin]]) where participants (miners) must solve a computationally expensive puzzle to propose the next block. This prevents attacks and ensures that the network reaches agreement even in an adversarial environment.
- A piece of data that is difficult to calculate but easy to verify
- First proposed to prevent [[Denial of Service (DoS) attacks]]
